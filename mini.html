How It Works

Smart Protection in
Four Simple Steps

Our AI works silently in the background, ensuring your loved ones are safe without intruding on their daily lives.

1
2
3
4

📷 Camera Captures Scene

A standard camera captures the room. The raw video never leaves the device — it's processed entirely on-device using edge computing for maximum privacy.

🦴 Pose Estimation Converts

AI instantly converts the video into an anonymous skeleton model using pose estimation. Only body joint positions are tracked — no face, no identity.

🧠 AI Analyzes Movement

Our deep learning model analyzes velocity, body angle, impact patterns, and post-fall stillness to distinguish real falls from sitting, bending, or playing.

🚨 3-Tier Alert Cascade

If a real fall is confirmed: instant notification to family → auto-call to caretaker → emergency services contacted automatically if no response.
